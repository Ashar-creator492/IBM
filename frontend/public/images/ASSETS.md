# Q-Route visual assets

These original assets were generated for this project with the built-in OpenAI
image generation tool on 2026-11-12. They are decorative artwork, not photographs
of a real mission or solver output. No external stock images are used. The UI
and route diagrams are independent React/SVG elements, not a flattened screenshot.

- `alpine-mountains.png`: original alpine scenery, used by the hero and live-lab CTA.
- `drone.png`: original white quadcopter product image with a transparent alpha channel.

## Final mountain prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: background photograph for a premium light website hero, landscape 3:2 composition. Create a beautiful photorealistic snowy alpine mountain range, pale powder-blue winter sky, layered atmospheric snowy peaks. Main dramatic triangular craggy peak at upper right-center, lower foreground white snowy ridges at bottom. Soft luminous diffuse daylight, subtle mist, very pale cool blue and silver-white color palette, crisp photography with restrained contrast, elegant airy art direction. No drone, no people, no text, no UI, no graphics, no borders. This is an original scenic photograph asset, not an entire website design.

## Final drone prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: isolated photographic cutout for a luxury light website hero. Generate one photorealistic premium white quadcopter drone floating in mid-air on a genuinely transparent background. Three-quarter front overhead angle: see white sculpted top fuselage and front-facing black gimbal camera below nose at lower right. Four arms and four full circular charcoal propeller safety guards with visible realistic propeller blades. Drone oriented diagonally lower-left to upper-right, rear rotor high at upper right, camera facing viewer slightly right. Pearlescent silver-white body, graphite arms and motors, subtle warm amber LED strips on front rotor guards, small vivid green indicator alongside camera. High-end commercial product photography, soft cool daylight matching pale snowy alpine backdrop. Entire drone visible with a little margin, wide horizontal 3:2 framing. No mountain, no floor, no backdrop, no text, no logo, no UI. Sharp physical materials, realistic engineering, avoid toy/cartoon style.
